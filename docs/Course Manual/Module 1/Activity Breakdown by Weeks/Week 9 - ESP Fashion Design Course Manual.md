# Module 1 — Week 9 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 9 title

**Introduction to Patternmaking English**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|9 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Introdução à Modelagem, introductory sewing, garment construction basics|
|Main ESP function|Understanding basic patternmaking vocabulary, measurements, pattern pieces, and simple instructions|
|Main student artifact|Basic Pattern Piece Description Card|
|App workload|75–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 9 purpose

Week 9 builds naturally from Week 8. Students already practiced sewing actions and process order. Now they apply that same process language to **introductory patternmaking**: measuring, marking, tracing, cutting, labeling, and identifying simple pattern pieces.

This week connects English directly to one of the most useful practical areas of a fashion design program. Students learn the words needed to understand simple patternmaking instructions and describe a basic pattern piece.

The main output is a **Basic Pattern Piece Description Card**. The student identifies one pattern piece, labels important information, and writes a short description of what the piece is for.

|Required assets|4–6 step images showing tracing and labeling a simple pattern piece|
|Reading level|A2|

## Reading text model

> **Trace a Simple Pattern Piece**  
> First, place the pattern paper on the table. Then, put the pattern piece on the paper. Next, trace the outside line with a pencil. After that, mark the grainline and the fold line. Then, add the seam allowance. Finally, label the pattern piece and check the measurements.

## Reading questions

|Question type|Example|
|---|---|
|sequence|Put the steps in the correct order.|
|multiple choice|What do you mark after tracing the outside line?|
|true/false|First, cut the fabric.|
|vocabulary check|“Trace” means: traçar / costurar / vestir / passar|
|detail question|What do you label at the end?|

## Expected correct sequence

1. Place the pattern paper on the table.
    
2. Put the pattern piece on the paper.
    
3. Trace the outside line with a pencil.
    
4. Mark the grainline and the fold line.
    
5. Add the seam allowance.
    
6. Label the pattern piece.
    
7. Check the measurements.

# Activity 7 — Basic Pattern Piece Description Card

|Field|Specification|
|---|---|
|Activity purpose|Produce the main Week 9 written artifact|
|Activity type|Guided writing|
|Platform category|`writing`|
|Estimated time|15–25 minutes|
|Submission mode|`text_response`|
|Feedback|hybrid: automated correction suggestions + teacher visibility|
|Target length|70–100 words|
|Required assets|student-selected pattern piece image from gallery or teacher-provided image|
|Student-facing language|Portuguese instructions; English sentence frames|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escolha uma peça de molde da galeria. Escreva uma descrição simples em inglês. Diga que peça é, para que roupa ela serve, quais marcações aparecem no molde e quais ações você faz para preparar essa peça.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|pattern piece name|This is a ___ pattern piece.|
|garment purpose|This piece is for a ___.|
|body/garment area|It is for the front/back/sleeve/waist/pocket.|
|markings|It has a grainline / fold line / notch / dart / seam allowance.|
|process action|First, trace the pattern. Then, mark the ___.|
|label|Label the piece with the name and size.|
|check|Finally, check the measurements.|

## Model answer 1 — skirt front

> This is a skirt front pattern piece. This piece is for a skirt. It has a waistline, a side seam, a grainline, and seam allowance. It also has one notch on the side seam. First, trace the pattern on paper. Then, mark the grainline and the notch. Finally, label the piece and check the waist measurement.

## Model answer 2 — sleeve

> This is a sleeve pattern piece. This piece is for a blouse or jacket. It has an armhole curve, a grainline, seam allowance, and notches. First, trace the outside line. Then, mark the grainline and the notches. Finally, label the pattern piece and check the sleeve length.

|2|correct measurement/marking vocabulary|
|3|clear use of “This piece is for…”|
|4|clear use of process verbs: trace, mark, label, check|
|5|correct article use: a pattern piece, the grainline|
|6|correct sequence words|
|7|sentence boundaries and capitalization|
|8|spelling of patternmaking terms|

# Week 9 course-manual text summary

**Week 9 introduces introductory patternmaking English. Students learn basic words for patterns, pattern pieces, tools, body and garment measurements, and pattern markings such as grainline, fold line, seam allowance, notch, dart, center front, center back, and side seam. The online work includes pattern vocabulary, measurement and mark labeling, a short patternmaking process reading, listening to patternmaking instructions, phrase-frame practice, and guided writing. The central language focus is “This is a ___ pattern piece,” “This piece is for…,” “Measure…,” “Mark…,” “Trace…,” “Cut…,” “Label…,” and “Check…”. Students produce a Basic Pattern Piece Description Card that supports introductory modeling/patternmaking coursework and prepares them for later sketch, prototype, and technical-spec writing. The teacher-led session reinforces pronunciation, pattern-piece labeling, and a short supported explanation of a pattern piece.**
