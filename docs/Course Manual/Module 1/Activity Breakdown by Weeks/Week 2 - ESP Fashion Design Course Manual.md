# Module 1 — Week 2 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 2 title

**The Fashion Studio**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|2 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Introductory sewing, introductory patternmaking, observation drawing, creativity lab|
|Main ESP function|Naming tools, places, materials, and basic studio actions|
|Main student artifact|“My Studio Kit” vocabulary card + short studio routine description|
|App workload|70–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 2 purpose

Week 2 moves from student identity into the physical environment of fashion design study: the studio, classroom, tools, work surfaces, and basic actions. The student learns the words they need to understand simple studio instructions and describe what they use in early design coursework.

This week should be highly visual. The platform should use images of tools, fabric, sketches, rulers, mannequins, sewing machines, pattern paper, scissors, needles, thread, and a worktable.

## Week 2 learning outcomes

|Outcome type|By the end of Week 2, the student can…|
|---|---|
|Studio vocabulary|recognize and name common fashion studio tools and objects|
|Listening|understand short classroom/studio instructions involving basic tools|
|Reading / visual reading|identify tools and actions from image-based prompts|
|Writing|write 5–8 simple sentences about what they use in the studio|
|Grammar / phrases|use “I use…,” “I need…,” “This is…,” “These are…,” and basic imperatives|
|Feedback routine|revise one sentence using corrected word order or missing article feedback|
|Teacher session|say what is in their studio kit and what they use it for|

## Core vocabulary for Week 2

|Category|Target words / phrases|
|---|---|
|Studio places|studio, classroom, table, desk, wall, board, sewing area|
|Tools|scissors, ruler, measuring tape, pencil, eraser, needle, thread, pins|
|Fashion tools|mannequin, sewing machine, sketchbook, pattern paper, fabric swatch|
|Materials|fabric, paper, thread, button, zipper|
|Actions|draw, cut, measure, fold, pin, sew, write, check, choose|
|Classroom routine|listen, look, open, close, take, put, use, submit, review|
|Object phrases|a pair of scissors, a ruler, a sewing machine, a piece of fabric, a sketchbook|

## Main grammar / phrase targets

|Function|Sentence frames|
|---|---|
|Naming objects|This is a ruler. / These are pins.|
|Saying what you use|I use scissors. / I use a ruler to measure.|
|Saying what you need|I need fabric, paper, and thread.|
|Giving simple instructions|Cut the paper. / Fold the fabric. / Measure the waist.|
|Locating objects|The scissors are on the table. / The fabric is near the sewing machine.|
|Connecting tool and purpose|I use a pencil to draw. / I use scissors to cut fabric.|

|Required assets|one short audio file, optional transcript revealed after attempt|
|Audio length|50–70 seconds|
|Replay rule|allow 2–3 plays|
|Language level|A2, clear, slow, direct|

## Audio script model

> Good morning. Today, we are working in the fashion studio. Take your sketchbook, pencil, ruler, and measuring tape. Put the fabric on the table. Look at the pattern paper. Draw one straight line with your ruler. Measure the fabric. Fold the fabric carefully. Then use pins to hold the fabric. Do not sew yet. First, check your measurements.

For the course manual, Codex can use this as the base script and later generate audio from it.

## Listening questions

|Question type|Example|
|---|---|
|multiple choice|What should the student take first?|
|sequence|Put the steps in order: measure, fold, pin, check|
|image choice|Which object is “measuring tape”?|
|true/false|The teacher says to sew immediately.|
|fill blank|Put the fabric on the ___.|

## Expected correct sequence

1. Take sketchbook, pencil, ruler, and measuring tape.
    
2. Put the fabric on the table.
    
3. Look at the pattern paper.
    
4. Draw a straight line.
    
5. Measure the fabric.
    
6. Fold the fabric.
    
7. Use pins.
    
8. Check measurements.

# Activity 6 — My Studio Kit

|Field|Specification|
|---|---|
|Activity purpose|Produce the main Week 2 written artifact|
|Activity type|Guided writing|
|Platform category|`writing`|
|Estimated time|15–20 minutes|
|Submission mode|`text_response`|
|Feedback|hybrid: automated correction suggestions + teacher visibility|
|Target length|60–90 words|
|Required assets|optional image prompt of studio kit objects|
|Student-facing language|Portuguese instructions; English sentence frames|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escreva um pequeno texto em inglês sobre o seu kit de ateliê. Diga quais ferramentas e materiais você usa, onde você usa essas coisas e para que elas servem. Use as frases-modelo abaixo.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|identity connection|I am a fashion design student.|
|tools|In my studio kit, I have ___.|
|materials|I use fabric, paper, and ___.|
|purpose|I use ___ to ___.|
|location|I work at a table / in the studio / in class.|
|preference|My favorite tool is ___ because ___.|

## Model answer

> I am a fashion design student. In my studio kit, I have scissors, a pencil, a ruler, a measuring tape, pins, and a sketchbook. I use a pencil to draw sketches. I use scissors to cut paper and fabric. I use a measuring tape to measure. I work at a table in the studio. My favorite tool is my sketchbook because I like drawing ideas.

## Feedback targets

|Priority|Feedback focus|
|---|---|
|1|correct use of Week 2 vocabulary|
|2|clear “I use ___ to ___” phrases|
|3|articles: a ruler, a pencil, a sewing machine|
|4|plural nouns: scissors, pins, sketches|
|5|sentence boundaries|
|6|spelling of key vocabulary|

|Clarity|3|Meaning is understandable|
|Target phrases|2|Uses “I use ___ to ___” correctly|

Total: 10 points.

# Week 2 course-manual text summary

**Week 2 introduces the fashion studio as the student’s first professional/academic English environment. Students learn to identify common studio tools, materials, and workspaces, then connect those objects to simple actions such as draw, cut, measure, fold, pin, and sew. The online work is visual and routine-based: students match images to words, find objects in a studio scene, listen to short studio instructions, practice tool-action phrases, and write a short “My Studio Kit” text. The teacher-led session reinforces pronunciation, checks app progress, and gives each student a supported speaking opportunity using the frame “I use ___ to ___.”**
