# Module 1 — Week 8 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 8 title

**Sewing Actions**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|8 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Introdução à Costura, introductory studio practice, fabric handling, garment construction basics|
|Main ESP function|Understanding and writing simple sewing-process instructions|
|Main student artifact|Simple Sewing Process Card|
|App workload|75–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 8 purpose

Week 8 turns vocabulary into process language. Students have already learned studio tools, garments, garment parts, sketches, visual description, and fabrics. Now they learn how to describe what happens in a basic sewing sequence.

The main language focus is **action + object + sequence**:

- Cut the fabric.
    
- Fold the edge.
    
- Pin the seam.
    
- Sew a straight line.
    
- Press the hem.
    
- Check the stitches.
    

This week is important because sewing instructions are one of the clearest places where English becomes immediately useful. Students can read simple tutorials, understand short studio directions, describe their own process, and prepare for later patternmaking/prototyping language.

The main output is a **Simple Sewing Process Card**: a short step-by-step description of a basic sewing task.

|Function|Sentence frames|
|---|---|
|Giving simple instructions|Cut the fabric. / Fold the edge. / Pin the seam.|
|Sequencing|First, measure the fabric. Then, mark the line.|
|Tool + action|Use scissors to cut the fabric.|
|Action + object|Sew a straight line. / Press the hem.|
|Location|Sew along the line. / Fold at the edge.|
|Checking quality|Check the seam. / The stitch is straight.|
|Process summary|This process has six steps.|
|Preference/reflection|I think this step is important because ___.|

## Language notes for Codex

|Language point|Instructional note|
|---|---|
|Imperatives|Main structure: verb first, no subject needed|
|Sequence words|Keep stable order: first, then, next, after that, finally|
|“use ___ to ___”|Recycle Week 2 structure: “Use scissors to cut fabric”|
|Object nouns|Encourage complete phrases: cut the fabric, fold the edge, pin the seam|
|Location phrases|Use visually clear examples: along the line, on the edge, at the hem|
|“press”|Explain through context as a sewing action with an iron|
|Sentence length|Keep student output short and step-based|

## Reading text model

> **Make a Simple Hem Sample**  
> First, cut a small rectangle of fabric. Then, fold one edge of the fabric. Next, pin the fold carefully. After that, sew a straight line near the folded edge. Then, remove the pins. Finally, press the hem with an iron and check the stitches.

1. Cut a small rectangle of fabric.

3. Pin the fold carefully.
    
4. Sew a straight line near the folded edge.
    
5. Remove the pins.
    
6. Press the hem with an iron.
    
7. Check the stitches.

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escreva um cartão de processo em inglês para uma tarefa simples de costura. Use frases curtas e palavras de sequência: first, then, next, after that, finally. Você pode descrever uma barra simples, uma amostra de costura reta ou uma preparação de tecido.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|process title|Simple Hem Sample / Straight Stitch Sample / Fabric Preparation|
|first step|First, ___.|
|sequence steps|Then, ___. / Next, ___. / After that, ___.|
|tool phrase|Use ___ to ___.|
|sewing action|Sew ___.|
|final check|Finally, check ___.|
|optional reflection|This step is important because ___.|

## Model answer 1 — simple hem sample

> **Simple Hem Sample**  
> First, cut a small rectangle of fabric. Then, fold one edge. Next, pin the fold carefully. After that, sew a straight line near the edge. Then, remove the pins. Finally, press the hem with an iron and check the stitches.

## Model answer 2 — straight stitch sample

> **Straight Stitch Sample**  
> First, place the fabric on the table. Then, mark a straight line with a pencil. Next, put the fabric in the sewing machine. After that, sew along the line. Finally, cut the thread and check the stitch. The line should be straight and clean.

# Week 8 course-manual text summary

**Week 8 introduces sewing-action language and simple process writing. Students learn verbs such as cut, fold, pin, sew, stitch, press, measure, mark, trace, and check, then connect those actions to familiar tools and materials from earlier weeks. The online work includes action vocabulary, tool-action matching, a short process reading, listening-and-sequencing practice, sequence-word practice, and guided writing. The central language focus is imperative instructions and process markers: “First, measure the fabric. Then, mark the line. Next, cut the fabric. Finally, check the seam.” Students produce a Simple Sewing Process Card that describes a basic sewing task in 6–8 short steps. The teacher-led session reinforces pronunciation, tool-action language, and spoken explanation of a simple sewing sequence.**
