# Module 1 — Week 5 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 5 title

**Body, Figure, and Observation Drawing**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|5 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Desenho de Observação e Desenho da Figura Humana; fashion sketching; introductory design description|
|Main ESP function|Describing the fashion figure, pose, basic body reference points, and sketch features|
|Main student artifact|Labeled fashion figure/sketch + short figure description|
|App workload|70–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 5 purpose

Week 5 connects English to **observation drawing and fashion figure work**. Students have already learned student identity, studio tools, garment names, and garment parts. Now they learn the language needed to describe the human figure as used in fashion sketching: head, neck, shoulders, arms, waist, hips, legs, pose, front view, side view, outline, proportion, and silhouette.

This week supports drawing coursework without requiring the online platform to become a drawing app. The app can use prepared images of fashion figures, croquis, poses, and simple sketch examples. Students describe and label visual material, then write a short description of a selected sketch or figure.

The main output is a **Fashion Figure Description Card**: a labeled image plus a short written description of the figure, pose, garment, and key visual features.

## Vocabulary notes for Codex

|Word / phrase|Design-course note|
|---|---|
|figure|Use for a drawn or represented body, especially in fashion drawing|
|model|Can refer to the person/figure wearing the garment; use simply|
|croquis|Keep as a fashion drawing term students may already know; explain as “fashion sketch”|
|pose|The position of the figure|
|proportion|The relation of body parts in drawing; introduce lightly|
|silhouette|The overall shape of the body/garment combination|
|outline|The outside line or contour|
|waist vs. waistband|Week 5 uses **waist** as body reference; Week 4 used **waistband** as garment part|
|hip|Body reference point; useful for sketching and patternmaking later|
|foot / feet|Introduce singular/plural only passively; use “foot” in labels|

# Main grammar / phrase targets

|Function|Sentence frames|
|---|---|
|Naming figure parts|This is the head. / These are the shoulders.|
|Describing the figure|The figure is standing.|
|Describing the pose|The pose is simple. / The pose is relaxed.|
|Describing view|This is a front view. / This is a side view.|
|Describing clothing on figure|The figure is wearing a dress.|
|Connecting garment to body|The skirt is at the waist. / The hem is below the knee.|
|Describing silhouette|The silhouette is long and simple.|
|Describing sketch style|The outline is simple. / The lines are long.|
|Preference / observation|I like the pose because it is clear.|

## Language notes for Codex

|Language point|Instructional note|
|---|---|
|“The figure is…”|Main frame for pose/view description|
|“The figure is wearing…”|Use for garments on the sketch/model|
|“This is the…”|Use for labels on body-reference points|
|“These are the…”|Use for plural body parts: shoulders, arms, hands, legs|
|above/below|Use only with obvious visual examples: above the knee, below the knee|
|adjective placement|English adjective before noun: simple outline, long silhouette, relaxed pose|
|respectful body language|Keep wording focused on drawing, pose, proportion, garment fit, and silhouette|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escolha uma imagem de croqui ou figura de moda da galeria. Escreva uma descrição em inglês. Descreva a pose, a vista, a roupa, a silhueta e um detalhe que você observa no desenho.

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|image type|This is a fashion figure / sketch / croquis.|
|pose|The figure is standing / walking / sitting.|
|view|This is a front view / side view / back view.|
|garment|The figure is wearing ___.|
|garment detail|The garment has ___.|
|silhouette|The silhouette is ___.|
|observation|I notice ___.|
|preference|I like the ___ because ___.|

## Model answer 1 — front-view dress sketch

> This is a fashion figure in a front view. The figure is standing. She is wearing a simple blue dress. The dress has short sleeves and a round neckline. The hem is below the knee. The silhouette is long and straight. I like the outline because it is clear and simple.

## Model answer 2 — side-view jacket and pants sketch

> This is a side-view sketch. The figure is wearing a black jacket and wide pants. The pose is relaxed. The jacket has long sleeves and pockets. The pants are loose. The silhouette is modern. I like this sketch because the body line is simple and clear.

# Week 5 course-manual text summary

**Week 5 connects English to observation drawing and fashion figure work. Students learn basic body-reference vocabulary, figure/sketch terms, pose language, and simple visual-description phrases. The online work includes body/figure vocabulary practice, image labeling, listening to pose descriptions, reading short sketch captions, phrase-frame practice, and guided writing. The central language focus is “The figure is…,” “The figure is wearing…,” “This is a front/side view,” and “The silhouette is…”. Students produce a Fashion Figure Description Card describing a selected sketch or croquis. The teacher-led session reinforces pronunciation, figure labeling, and a short supported explanation of a fashion sketch.**
