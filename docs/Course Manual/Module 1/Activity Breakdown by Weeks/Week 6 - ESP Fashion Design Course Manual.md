# Module 1 — Week 6 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 6 title

**Color, Shape, and Line**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|6 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Drawing, creativity lab, introductory design studies, early color/design vocabulary|
|Main ESP function|Describing visual elements in fashion sketches, garments, and looks|
|Main student artifact|Color + Shape Look Description|
|App workload|75–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 6 purpose

Week 6 gives students the English they need to describe **what a design looks like visually** before they get into deeper design rationale. Students have already learned garment names, garment parts, and figure/sketch language. Now they add color, shape, line, and silhouette vocabulary.

This week supports early fashion drawing and creativity coursework. It also prepares students for later modules on moodboards, collections, styling, textiles, visual merchandising, and portfolio captions.

The main output is a **Color + Shape Look Description**: the student chooses or is assigned a look/sketch/image and describes its colors, shapes, lines, silhouette, and overall visual impression.

# Week 6 learning outcomes

|Outcome type|By the end of Week 6, the student can…|
|---|---|
|Color vocabulary|name basic colors and simple color families in English|
|Visual vocabulary|recognize and use words for shape, line, and silhouette|
|Reading / visual reading|match visual descriptions to looks or sketches|
|Listening|identify a look from a spoken description of color, shape, and line|
|Writing|write a 70–100 word visual description of a look or sketch|
|Grammar / phrases|use adjective order in simple fashion descriptions: “a long black dress,” “a loose white shirt”|
|Feedback routine|revise sentences with improved adjective order and clearer visual vocabulary|
|Teacher session|describe the colors, shapes, and lines of one look using prepared frames|

# Main grammar / phrase targets

|Function|Sentence frames|
|---|---|
|Naming colors|The dress is blue. / The jacket is black.|
|Describing color quality|The color is bright. / The palette is soft.|
|Describing shape|The silhouette is long and straight.|
|Describing fit|The shirt is loose. / The pants are fitted.|
|Describing line|The sketch has curved lines.|
|Describing contrast|The look has black and white contrast.|
|Describing palette|The color palette is warm and soft.|
|Adjective order|This is a long black dress.|
|Visual preference|I like the color palette because it is simple.|

## Language notes for Codex

|Language point|Instructional note|
|---|---|
|adjective order|Keep it simple: size/shape before color before garment: “a long black dress”|
|color as adjective|“a blue skirt,” “black pants,” “a white shirt”|
|singular/plural|“The dress is…” vs. “The pants are…”|
|“palette”|Use “The color palette is…” for design description|
|“look”|Use as a fashion noun meaning outfit/visual composition|
|“line”|Keep examples visual and concrete: straight, curved, vertical, horizontal|
|“silhouette”|Use with shape adjectives: long, wide, narrow, straight, loose, fitted|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escolha um look ou croqui da galeria. Escreva uma descrição visual em inglês. Descreva a paleta de cores, as peças de roupa, a silhueta, as linhas ou formas e a impressão geral do look.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|look/sketch identification|This is a look / sketch / fashion figure.|
|color palette|The color palette is ___.|
|garments|The figure is wearing ___.|
|garment color|The ___ is ___.|
|silhouette|The silhouette is ___.|
|line/shape|The lines are ___. / The shape is ___.|
|visual impression|The look is simple / modern / playful / classic.|
|preference|I like the ___ because ___.|

## Model answer 1 — neutral modern look

> This is a modern look. The color palette is neutral. The figure is wearing a long black dress and a short white jacket. The silhouette is long and straight. The lines are simple. The look has black and white contrast. I like the palette because it is classic and clear.

## Model answer 2 — bright playful look

> This is a bright and playful look. The figure is wearing a yellow cropped top and wide blue pants. The color palette is bright. The pants are loose, and the top is fitted. The silhouette is wide and modern. I like the colors because they are strong and fun.

|3|correct adjective order|

|5|correct singular/plural with pants/shorts/jeans|
|6|clear sentence boundaries|
|7|spelling of visual-design vocabulary|

# Week 6 course-manual text summary

**Week 6 introduces color, shape, line, and silhouette as core visual-description language for fashion design students. Students learn basic color vocabulary, palette language, visual shape words, line types, and simple silhouette terms such as loose, fitted, straight, flared, oversized, and cropped. The online work includes color and palette practice, shape/line/silhouette vocabulary, short reading descriptions, listening identification, adjective-order practice, and guided writing. The central language focus is “The color palette is…,” “The silhouette is…,” “The lines are…,” and adjective order in phrases such as “a long black dress.” Students produce a Color + Shape Look Description that can later support portfolio captions, moodboards, styling notes, and collection concepts. The teacher-led session reinforces pronunciation, visual-description confidence, and spoken use of color/shape/line language.**
