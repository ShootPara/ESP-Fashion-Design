## Week 14 title

**Mini Design Board**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|

## Week 14 purpose

Week 14 is the final integration week for **Studio English Foundations I**. Students combine the language from the entire module into one small but useful design artifact: a **Mini Design Board in English**.

The board should be simple enough for A2 students, but meaningful enough to support their university coursework. It is not a full professional portfolio page yet. It is a first English-language design board with:

- one selected sketch or garment image
    
- 8–12 labels
    
- a garment/look description
    
- a fabric/material note
    
- a design-detail note
    
- an inspiration/rationale note
    
- a corrected final version after feedback
    
- a short teacher-session presentation
    

This week turns the previous weekly artifacts into a final, reusable student product.

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Agora escreva os textos finais da sua mini prancha de design em inglês. Use frases curtas. Inclua título, descrição do look, nota de tecido/detalhe, nota de inspiração e comentário final.

## Required sections

|Section|Expected length|Required content|
|---|--:|---|
|Title|2–6 words|simple design title|
|Labels|8–12 labels|garment, parts, details, fabric, color/mood|
|Garment/look description|50–70 words|sketch, figure, garment, silhouette, color|
|Fabric/detail note|35–50 words|fabric idea + one or two details|
|Inspiration note|35–50 words|source + choice + mood|
|Final comment|1 sentence|overall look or revision statement|

## Section frames

### Title

> **[Adjective / Theme] + [Garment or Look]**

Examples:

- Soft Ocean Dress
    
- Urban Denim Jacket
    
- Bright Movement Look
    
- Calm Green Skirt
    
- Modern Streetwear Outfit
    

### Garment/look description

> This board shows ___.  
> The figure is ___.  
> The figure is wearing ___.  
> The color palette is ___.  
> The silhouette is ___.

### Fabric/detail note

> I imagine this garment in ___.  
> The fabric looks ___.  
> The main detail is ___.  
> It is functional/decorative because ___.

### Inspiration note

> This design is inspired by ___.  
> I chose ___ because ___.  
> This creates a ___ mood.

### Final comment

> The overall look is ___.  
> I revised ___ to make it clearer.

## Model answer 1 — complete board text

### Title

**Urban Denim Jacket**

### Labels

denim jacket; white top; black pants; zipper; patch pockets; long sleeves; structured fabric; neutral palette; straight silhouette; city inspiration

### Garment/look description

> This board shows a fashion sketch in a front view. The figure is wearing a short denim jacket, a white top, and black pants. The color palette is neutral, with blue, black, and white. The silhouette is straight and casual.

### Fabric/detail note

> I imagine the jacket in structured denim. The main details are the zipper and patch pockets on the front. They are functional because they open the jacket and hold small objects.

### Inspiration note

> This design is inspired by the city. I chose denim and neutral colors because they look practical and modern. The overall look is urban, comfortable, and strong.

### Final comment

> I revised my detail note to make the function clearer.

# Week 14 course-manual text summary

**Week 14 is the final integration week for Studio English Foundations I. Students create a Mini Design Board in English that combines the main language from the module: garment names, garment parts, figure/sketch description, color palette, silhouette, fabric, design details, inspiration, rationale, feedback, and revision. The online work includes cumulative vocabulary review, reading a model mini design board, listening to a model presentation, building labels and captions, drafting final text sections, completing a revision checklist, and submitting the final artifact. The central language focus is “This board shows…,” “The figure is wearing…,” “The color palette is…,” “I imagine this garment in…,” “The main detail is…,” “This design is inspired by…,” “I chose ___ because…,” and “The overall look is…”. Students finish the module with a reusable English-language design artifact and a short supported presentation in the teacher-led session.**
