# Module 1 — Week 10 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 10 title

**Describing a Sketch**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|10 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Drawing, observation drawing, introductory sewing, introductory patternmaking, creativity lab|
|Main ESP function|Combining garment, figure, color, fabric, and detail vocabulary into one sketch description|
|Main student artifact|Fashion Sketch Description|
|App workload|80–95 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 10 purpose

Week 10 is the first major consolidation week. Students have already learned the vocabulary and sentence frames for:

- student identity and course routine
    
- studio tools
    
- garments and garment categories
    
- garment parts
    
- figure, pose, and croquis
    
- color, shape, line, and silhouette
    
- fabric and texture
    
- sewing actions
    
- basic patternmaking language
    

Now students use those pieces together to describe a fashion sketch in a more complete way.

The main output is a **Fashion Sketch Description**: a 90–130 word guided description of one sketch or croquis, including the figure/pose, garment, color palette, silhouette, fabric idea, garment details, and one simple design comment.

This week should feel like a milestone. The student is no longer writing isolated vocabulary sentences. They are producing a short text that resembles a basic portfolio caption or class presentation note.

# Activity 5 — Organize the Description

|Field|Specification|
|---|---|
|Activity purpose|Teach students how to organize a longer sketch description|
|Activity type|Paragraph-order / sentence-order task|
|Platform category|`reading` / `practice`|
|Estimated time|10–12 minutes|
|Submission mode|quiz / practice|
|Feedback|auto-scored|
|Required assets|optional sketch image|
|Reuse potential|high; supports all later writing tasks|

## Target organization

Students should learn a predictable order:

1. Identify the sketch/image.
    
2. Describe the figure or pose.
    
3. Describe the garment/outfit.
    
4. Describe colors and silhouette.
    
5. Describe details and fabric idea.
    
6. Add a design comment or preference.
    

## Sentence ordering model

Students receive scrambled sentences and put them in a logical order.

### Scrambled sentences

- The color palette is neutral, with black, white, and gray.
    
- This is a fashion sketch in a front view.
    
- The overall look is simple and modern.
    
- The figure is wearing a long coat, a white shirt, and black pants.
    
- The coat has long sleeves, pockets, and a straight silhouette.
    
- I imagine the coat in a structured wool fabric.
    

### Correct order

1. This is a fashion sketch in a front view.
    
2. The figure is wearing a long coat, a white shirt, and black pants.
    
3. The color palette is neutral, with black, white, and gray.
    
4. The coat has long sleeves, pockets, and a straight silhouette.
    
5. I imagine the coat in a structured wool fabric.
    
6. The overall look is simple and modern.
    

## Exercise formats

|Exercise|Example|
|---|---|
|sentence order|put 6 sentences in logical order|
|choose first sentence|Which sentence should start the description?|
|choose final sentence|Which sentence gives the final design comment?|
|category label|“The fabric looks flowy” → fabric idea|
|missing sentence|Choose the best sentence to complete the paragraph|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escolha um croqui ou sketch da galeria. Escreva uma descrição completa em inglês. Descreva a figura, a pose, a roupa, a paleta de cores, a silhueta, os detalhes, uma ideia de tecido e um comentário final sobre o design.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|sketch identification|This is a fashion sketch in a front/side view.|
|figure/pose|The figure is standing / walking / sitting.|
|garment/outfit|The figure is wearing ___.|

|silhouette|The silhouette is ___.|
|garment details|The garment has ___.|
|fabric idea|I imagine this garment in ___. / The fabric looks ___.|
|design comment|The main feature is ___. / The overall look is ___.|

## Model answer 1 — denim jacket look

> This is a fashion sketch in a front view. The figure is standing and wearing a short denim jacket, a white top, and black pants. The color palette is neutral. The jacket has long sleeves, buttons, and two pockets on the front. The silhouette is straight and casual. I imagine the jacket in structured denim. The main feature is the jacket because it is simple and functional. The overall look is modern and comfortable.

## Model answer 2 — soft dress look

> This is a fashion sketch of a long dress. The figure is standing in a relaxed pose. The dress has short sleeves, a round neckline, and a hem below the knee. The color palette is soft, with light blue and white. The silhouette is long and loose. The fabric looks lightweight and flowy. I chose this fabric idea because it looks comfortable. The overall look is simple, soft, and elegant.

## Model answer 3 — bright pants look

> This is a colorful fashion sketch. The figure is wearing a cropped yellow top and wide pink pants. The pose is confident, with one hand on the hip. The color palette is bright and playful. The pants have a high waistband and large pockets. I imagine the pants in lightweight cotton. The main feature is the wide silhouette. The overall look is creative and modern.

# Week 10 course-manual text summary

**Week 10 is the first major integration week in Studio English Foundations I. Students combine vocabulary and sentence frames from previous weeks to describe a complete fashion sketch. The week reviews garment names, garment parts, figure and pose language, color/palette vocabulary, silhouette and line vocabulary, fabric and texture language, and basic construction terms. The online work includes vocabulary review, reading model sketch descriptions, listening to spoken sketch descriptions, organizing sentences into a logical paragraph, phrase-frame practice, guided writing, and targeted revision. The central language focus is “The figure is wearing…,” “The garment has…,” “The color palette is…,” “The silhouette is…,” “I imagine this garment in…,” and “The overall look is…”. Students produce a Fashion Sketch Description that resembles a basic portfolio caption or class presentation note. The teacher-led session reinforces description structure, pronunciation, and a short supported presentation of the student’s selected sketch.**
