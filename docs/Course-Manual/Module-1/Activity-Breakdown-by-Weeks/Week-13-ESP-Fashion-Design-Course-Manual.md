# Module 1 — Week 13 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 13 title

**Giving Simple Design Feedback**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|13 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Creativity lab, drawing, introductory design studies, sewing/patternmaking reflection, peer critique|
|Main ESP function|Giving simple, constructive feedback on a garment, sketch, design detail, or design idea|
|Main student artifact|Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan|
|App workload|75–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 13 purpose

Week 13 prepares students to talk about design work with other people. Up to this point, students have described garments, garment parts, figures, sketches, color, fabric, construction, pattern pieces, design details, and inspiration. Now they learn how to respond to someone else’s design in English.

This week is also a preparation week for the final Week 14 mini design board. Students practice identifying what works, explaining why, and suggesting one improvement using simple, respectful, reusable phrases.

The main output is a **Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan**. The student reviews a look/sketch/design board image and writes short feedback:

- one positive observation
    
- one reason
    
- one suggestion
    
- one useful vocabulary upgrade
    
- one revision plan sentence

## Reading text models

> The strongest part is the silhouette. The wide pants make the look modern and comfortable. Maybe you could use a structured fabric for the jacket to create more contrast.

> I like the pockets because they are functional and decorative. The sketch is clear, but the caption needs more information. You could add one sentence about the inspiration source.

# Activity 6 — Improve the Feedback

|Field|Specification|
|---|---|
|Activity purpose|Help students upgrade vague comments into useful design feedback|
|Activity type|Rewrite / choose stronger comment|
|Platform category|`practice` / `writing`|
|Estimated time|10–12 minutes|
|Submission mode|quiz or short text|
|Feedback|auto-scored for MC; completion/AI-supported for rewrite|
|Required assets|optional sketch images|
|Reuse potential|high; supports feedback quality and final revision work|

## Student-facing concept

Students compare weak/basic comments with stronger, more useful feedback. They learn that useful feedback includes a focus area and a reason or suggestion.

## Example transformations

|Basic comment|Stronger feedback|
|---|---|
|I like it.|I like the color palette because it is soft and calm.|
|Good dress.|The dress works well because the silhouette is long and elegant.|
|Add detail.|You could add embroidery near the neckline.|
|Change color.|Maybe you could add more contrast with a darker color.|
|Nice fabric.|The fabric looks lightweight and flowy, so it works well for the dress.|
|Bad caption.|The caption needs more information about the inspiration source.|
|The look is okay.|The strongest part is the wide silhouette, but the color palette could be clearer.|

## Exercise formats

|Exercise|Example|
|---|---|
|choose stronger feedback|pick the more useful comment|
|match vague to improved|“Nice color” → “I like the warm palette because…”|
|fill missing reason|I like the pockets because they are ___.|
|complete suggestion|You could add ___.|
|short rewrite|Rewrite: “I like it” with one design word and one reason.|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Observe um look, sketch ou mini design board da galeria. Escreva uma nota curta de feedback em inglês. Inclua um comentário positivo, uma razão, uma sugestão e uma frase de revisão. Use frases simples e respeitosas.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|positive comment|I like the ___.|
|reason|I like it because ___.|
|strength|The strongest part is ___.|
|suggestion|You could ___. / Maybe you could ___.|
|design focus|The color/fabric/detail/silhouette works well because ___.|
|revision plan|I will revise ___. / I will add/change/use ___.|
|final sentence|This will make the look/text/design ___.|

## Model answer 1 — feedback on soft dress

> I like the color palette because it is soft and calm. The flowy fabric works well with the long dress. The strongest part is the simple silhouette. Maybe you could add a small decorative detail near the neckline, like embroidery. I will revise my own design note by adding one sentence about fabric and mood.

## Model answer 2 — feedback on urban jacket

> I like the jacket because it is modern and practical. The zipper and large pockets are functional details. The neutral palette works well, but you could add more contrast with one bright color. I will revise my sketch description by making the main feature clearer. This will make the description stronger.

## Model answer 3 — feedback on bright look

> The strongest part is the bright color palette. It creates an energetic and playful mood. I like the wide pants because they show movement. Maybe you could simplify the caption and add one sentence about the inspiration source. I will revise my final design board by checking my captions and adding clearer reasons.

# Week 13 course-manual text summary

**Week 13 teaches students to give simple, constructive design feedback in English. Students learn feedback vocabulary and reusable critique phrases such as “I like… because…,” “The strongest part is…,” “This works well because…,” “You could…,” “Maybe you could…,” and “I will revise…”. The week focuses on positive comment, reason, suggestion, and revision plan as a predictable feedback structure. The online work includes phrase-bank practice, feedback-category sorting, reading peer comments, listening to spoken feedback, improving vague comments, phrase-frame practice, guided feedback writing, and targeted revision. Students produce a Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan that prepares them to revise their final Week 14 mini design board. The teacher-led session is a mini critique circle where students practice one positive comment and one suggestion using supported English frames.**
