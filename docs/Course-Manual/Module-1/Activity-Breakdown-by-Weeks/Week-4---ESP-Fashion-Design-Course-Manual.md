# Module 1 — Week 4 Detailed Breakdown

## Week 4 title

**Parts of a Garment**

## Position in module

|Field|Value|
|---|---|
|Module|Studio English Foundations I|
|Week|4 of 14|
|Student level|A2 English|
|Main university-course connection|Introductory sewing, introductory patternmaking, drawing, garment observation|
|Main ESP function|Identifying and describing visible garment parts|
|Main student artifact|Labeled garment image + short “Garment Details” description|
|App workload|70–90 minutes|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 minutes|

## Week 4 purpose

Week 4 builds directly from Week 3. Students already know common garment names and categories. Now they learn the visible parts and details of garments so they can describe what they draw, observe, sew, alter, or critique.

This week is especially important for fashion design students because garment-part vocabulary connects English to drawing, sewing, patternmaking, product description, technical description, and portfolio captions.

The main output is a **labeled garment image** plus a short written description using detail vocabulary: sleeves, collar, neckline, hem, seam, zipper, buttons, pockets, waistband, and similar terms.

## Vocabulary notes for Codex

|Word|Design-course note|
|---|---|
|seam|structural line where fabric pieces are joined|
|stitch|visible or actual sewing action/result|
|topstitching|visible stitching on the surface; useful later but can be introduced passively|
|dart|shaped fold used for fitting; useful for patternmaking but can stay image-supported|
|lining|inside layer of a garment|
|neckline|shape/opening around the neck|
|collar|attached detail around the neck; not every neckline has a collar|
|cuff|finished part at the end of a sleeve or pant leg|
|hem|finished lower edge of a garment|

# Main grammar / phrase targets

|Function|Sentence frames|
|---|---|
|Naming garment parts|This is the collar. / These are the sleeves.|
|Describing details|It has a collar. / It has two pockets.|
|Singular / plural detail|It has one zipper. / It has three buttons.|
|Location|The zipper is on the front.|
|More location|The hem is at the bottom.|
|There is / there are|There is a pocket on the front. / There are buttons on the shirt.|
|Simple design comment|The pockets are functional. / The collar is decorative.|
|Preference|I like the details because they are simple.|

## Language notes for Codex

|Language point|Instructional note|
|---|---|
|“It has”|Main phrase for A2 garment detail description|
|“There is/are”|Use for location: “There is a zipper on the front.”|
|singular/plural|button/buttons, pocket/pockets, sleeve/sleeves|
|always plural in normal use|pants, jeans, shorts|
|location phrases|Keep phrase bank stable: on the front, on the back, at the top, at the bottom, on the side|
|“the” for parts|Use “the collar,” “the hem,” “the zipper” when referring to a specific garment part|

|Feedback|hybrid: automated correction suggestions + teacher visibility|
|Target length|70–100 words|
|Required assets|student-selected garment image from app gallery or teacher-provided image|
|Student-facing language|Portuguese instructions; English sentence frames|

## Writing prompt

**Portuguese student instruction:**

> Escolha uma peça de roupa da galeria. Escreva uma descrição dos detalhes dessa peça em inglês. Diga que peça é, quais partes ela tem, onde ficam os detalhes e qual detalhe você acha mais interessante.

## Required content

|Required element|Sentence frame|
|---|---|
|garment name|This is a ___. / These are ___.|
|garment category|It is a top / bottom / dress / jacket / outerwear item.|
|parts/details|It has ___.|
|location|There is a ___ on the front/back.|
|more location|The ___ is at the top/bottom/side.|
|design comment|I like the ___ because ___.|

## Model answer 1 — jacket

> This is a black jacket. It is outerwear. It has long sleeves, a zipper, two pockets, and a simple collar. The zipper is on the front. The pockets are on the front, too. The hem is at the bottom. I like the zipper because it is functional and modern.

## Model answer 2 — dress

> This is a blue dress. It is simple and casual. It has short sleeves, a round neckline, and a zipper on the back. The neckline is at the top. The hem is at the bottom. I like the sleeves because they are simple and comfortable.

|2|correct use of “It has…”|
|3|correct use of “There is/There are…”|
|4|singular/plural detail nouns|
|5|location phrases: on the front, on the back, at the top, at the bottom|
|6|sentence boundaries|
|7|spelling of garment-part words|

# Week 4 course-manual text summary

**Week 4 introduces garment-part vocabulary and detail-focused description. Students learn to identify visible garment parts such as collar, neckline, sleeve, cuff, zipper, button, pocket, waistband, hem, seam, pleat, dart, and lining. The online work combines visual labeling, short listening descriptions, phrase-frame practice, and guided writing. The central language focus is “It has…,” “There is/There are…,” and simple location phrases such as “on the front,” “on the back,” “at the top,” and “at the bottom.” Students produce a labeled garment image and a short garment-detail description. The teacher-led session reinforces pronunciation, live labeling, and a short explanation of garment details using the student’s written artifact.**
