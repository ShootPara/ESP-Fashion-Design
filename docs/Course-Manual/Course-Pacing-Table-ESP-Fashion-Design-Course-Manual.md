## Course pacing table: English for Fashion Design

|UEG timing|ESP module title|Major-course alignment|14-week online focus|Teacher-led weekly session|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Year 1, Semester 1**|**Studio English Foundations I: Garments, Tools, and Classroom Language**|Desenho de Observação, Introdução à Costura, Introdução à Modelagem, Introdução aos Estudos do Design, Laboratório de Criatividade|garment names, body/figure vocabulary, studio verbs, basic descriptions, short listening instructions, labeled sketches|check comprehension, pronunciation of core terms, short “show your sketch” routines|
|**Year 1, Semester 2**|**Studio English Foundations II: Accessories, Sewing, and Simple Design Research**|Ateliê de Costura I, Desenho de Acessórios, História do Vestuário, Metodologia de Projeto, Modelagem Plana Feminina I, Pesquisa em Design e Moda|accessory descriptions, sewing-process sequencing, simple research summaries, visual comparison, short written design notes|review student design notes, guided Q&A, peer description practice|
|**Year 2, Semester 3**|**Fashion History and Visual Description**|Ateliê de Costura II, Desenho de Moda I, Estudos Cromáticos, História da Moda, Metodologia Científica, Modelagem Plana Feminina II|colors, silhouettes, historical periods, adapted readings, past-tense fashion descriptions, image-based comprehension|short oral descriptions of historical looks and color choices|
|**Year 2, Semester 4**|**Textiles, Sustainability, and Collection Concepts**|Cultura e Moda Brasileira, Design e Sustentabilidade, Estudo de Coleção de Moda, Moulage, Tecnologia e Materiais Têxteis|fabric vocabulary, texture/material comparison, sustainability claims, moodboard captions, collection concept paragraphs|moodboard walk-throughs, concept feedback, pronunciation of textile terms|
|**Year 3, Semester 5**|**Collection Planning, Styling, and Fashion Media**|Desenho Técnico, Design de Superfície, Gestão do Design, Mídias de Moda, Planejamento e Desenvolvimento de Coleção, Styling, Visual Merchandising|product descriptions, styling briefs, trend blurbs, collection planning language, visual merchandising descriptions|critique language, mini-pitches, discussion of target customer and styling choices|
|**Year 3, Semester 6**|**Professional Fashion Communication**|Empreendedorismo, Estágio, Fotografia, Ilustração de Moda, Marketing de Moda, Produção de Moda, Técnicas de Alfaiataria|internship emails, marketing copy, photoshoot briefs, production-process language, tailoring vocabulary, workplace listening|workplace roleplay, progress conference, feedback on emails and briefs|
|**Year 4, Semester 7**|**Creative Identity, Prototyping, and Project Presentation I**|Identidade Criativa, Produção de Eventos, Projeto Integralizador, Prototipagem, Trabalho de Curso I|project titles, problem/solution language, prototype descriptions, technical-spec vocabulary, event-planning language|guided TCC/project check-ins, oral rehearsal, clarification of project vocabulary|
|**Year 4, Semester 8**|**Portfolio, Retail, Ethics, and Final Project English**|Varejo de Moda, CAD, Gestão de Produção, Liderança, Ilustração Digital Avançada, Moda/Filosofia/Ética, Seminários, Trabalho de Curso II|portfolio captions, final-project abstract, slide text, retail/customer language, ethics/sustainability language, final written presentation kit|final pitch practice, Q&A rehearsal, teacher feedback on portfolio/TCC English|

The UEG fashion curriculum supports this 8-module sequence neatly: the official matrix is 8 semesters, minimum 4 years, with 2,580 total hours, and the course sequence moves from drawing/sewing/modeling foundations into history, textiles, sustainability, collection development, styling, marketing, internship, prototyping, retail, CAD, seminars, and final course work.

## Standard semester shape

|Semester band|Weeks|Course function|
|---|--:|---|
|University start buffer|Weeks 1–2|onboarding, placement check, app login, diagnostic vocabulary, light first tasks|
|Core ESP module|Weeks 3–16|14 weekly online units, each tied to current fashion coursework|
|Flex/recovery week|Week 17|catch-up, remediation, optional teacher conference, late submissions|
|University closing buffer|Weeks 18–19|final university pressure zone: portfolio/TCC/exams/closing work, light review only|

## Standard 14-week module rhythm

|Week type|Weeks|Online platform work|
|---|--:|---|
|Setup + vocabulary base|1–2|visual vocabulary, short listening, controlled writing|
|Skill-building cycle|3–6|reading/listening + auto-corrected comprehension + short writing|
|Applied coursework cycle|7–10|design-task writing, captions, summaries, process descriptions|
|Integration cycle|11–13|longer artifact: board, brief, paragraph set, portfolio page, abstract, etc.|
|Module close|14|review, feedback, corrected final submission, progress reflection|

## Weekly workload target

|Component|Weekly amount|
|---|--:|
|Listening task|10–15 min|
|Reading / visual reading task|10–20 min|
|Vocabulary / phrase practice|10–15 min|
|Writing task|15–30 min|
|Auto-feedback / correction review|5–10 min|
|Teacher-led session|45–60 min once per week|
