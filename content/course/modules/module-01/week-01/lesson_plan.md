# Week 1: Welcome to Fashion Design English

## Overview
Week 1 establishes the routine for the whole course and should feel welcoming, visual, and clearly tied to Fashion Design study. Students learn how the weekly cycle works, complete a light diagnostic, read and listen to simple model introductions, practice starter vocabulary, write a short profile, and review one corrected sentence before a teacher-led speaking session.

This gold-standard Week 1 is still accessible for mixed A2-B1 learners, but it is complete enough to show how future weeks should combine app-ready structure, bilingual student support, professional relevance, assessment guidance, and asset planning.

## Learning Objectives
- Identify the main parts of the weekly course routine.
- Recognize and use starter Fashion Design and course-routine vocabulary.
- Understand the main ideas and key details in one short reading and one short listening text.
- Write a 50-80 word student profile using guided sentence frames.
- Revise one sentence after correction feedback.
- Deliver a short oral introduction with teacher support.

## Professional Fashion-Design Context
The week connects English to first-semester Fashion Design coursework from the beginning. Students meet vocabulary linked to drawing, sewing, patternmaking, creativity, text production, studio work, and portfolio building. The language is not generic self-introduction practice. It is the language students need to explain who they are as design students, what they study, what kinds of fashion work interest them, and why English matters for class tasks and future professional communication.

## Language Objectives
- Use beginner-accessible identity frames such as "My name is ___," "I am a fashion design student," "I study ___," and "I want to learn English for ___."
- Recognize and use core terms such as fashion design, sketch, fabric, garment, sewing, patternmaking, studio, portfolio, feedback, and submit.
- Use simple preference and goal language such as "I like ___," "I am interested in ___," and "English can help me with ___."
- Notice common correction targets, especially missing articles, missing verbs, and verb patterns after like and want.

## Materials Needed
- One welcome visual or slide for the weekly routine.
- One reading-support image for the Ana profile.
- One short listening audio clip or teacher read-aloud script.
- A small image set for Week 1 vocabulary practice.
- Student writing space for a 50-80 word profile.
- Teacher pronunciation list for the live session.

## Suggested Sequence
1. `m01w01-a01` Start Here: students meet the weekly rhythm and complete a light diagnostic about interests and goals.
2. `m01w01-a02` Meet Ana: students read a short model profile and answer gist/detail questions.
3. `m01w01-a03` Listen: students hear a first-semester student introduction and identify classes, interests, and English goals.
4. `m01w01-a04` Vocabulary Starter: students connect words to visuals, Portuguese support, and sentence frames.
5. `m01w01-a05` Fashion Student Profile: students write a short reusable profile in English.
6. `m01w01-a06` Review and Improve: students focus on one corrected sentence and save a revision.
7. `m01w01-a07` Teacher Session: students practice pronunciation, give a short spoken introduction, and confirm that they understand the course routine.

## Differentiation Options
- Let learners who need more support keep the English sentence frames visible during reading, listening, writing, and speaking.
- Provide a reduced writing path with four required sentences before inviting one optional challenge sentence.
- Offer a visible word bank during listening for students who need extra comprehension support.
- Allow oral rehearsal in pairs before students write or speak in the teacher-led session.
- Challenge stronger learners to add one sentence about a specific design area such as sustainability, accessories, styling, or portfolio goals.

## Extension Options
- Ask students to add one sentence describing a favorite design object such as a sketch, garment, fabric, or accessory.
- Invite students to compare their profile with Ana's model profile and identify one similarity and one difference.
- During the live session, ask confident learners to respond to one follow-up question such as "What is your favorite area in fashion design?" or "Why do you want English for portfolio work?"

## Notes for Future App Implementation
Week 1 maps well to a clear onboarding flow with an orientation card, reading card, listening player, image-supported vocabulary practice, text-entry writing task, correction review card, and teacher-reviewed speaking checkpoint.

The future app should preserve:
- bilingual English and Brazilian Portuguese student-facing text
- visible sentence frames and success criteria
- teacher visibility into the profile draft and revision step
- audio fallback with transcript release after attempt
- teacher-session support notes, not just app-graded tasks

The vocabulary image set should be reusable in later weeks, especially for studio tools, garments, and visual-description tasks.
