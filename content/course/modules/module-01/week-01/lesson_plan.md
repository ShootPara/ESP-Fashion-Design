# Week 1: Welcome to Fashion Design English

## Overview
This compact Week 1 seed introduces the course rhythm and shows how English connects to first-semester Fashion Design study. Students meet course language they will see every week, read and listen to short model introductions, and produce a short guided student profile in English.

## Learning Objectives
- Identify key words connected to the course routine and Fashion Design study.
- Understand short model texts about a Fashion Design student.
- Write a short self-introduction using sentence frames.
- Prepare to say a simple oral introduction in a teacher-led session.

## Professional Fashion-Design Context
The week connects English learning to the students' early coursework in drawing, sewing, patternmaking, creativity, and portfolio-building. The language is useful for studio introductions, class participation, and early portfolio reflection.

## Language Objectives
- Use "I am," "I study," "I like," and "I want to learn" accurately in short sentences.
- Recognize and use words such as "fashion design," "sketch," and "portfolio."
- Understand the main idea and key details in short reading and listening texts.

## Materials Needed
- One welcome visual for reading support
- One short listening audio clip or teacher read-aloud version
- Student writing space for a 50-80 word profile

## Suggested Sequence
1. Welcome and course routine warmup
2. Short reading on a Fashion Design student
3. Short listening on a first-semester student introduction
4. Guided writing task: student profile
5. Teacher-led oral introduction follow-up

## Timing Estimate
The seed week targets about 65 minutes of asynchronous work plus a short teacher-led speaking check-in. The activity mix stays small so the structure is reviewable in Phase 1.

## Differentiation Options
- Offer the model profile as a sentence bank for learners who need more support.
- Let stronger learners add one extra sentence about a specific fashion interest or future career goal.
- Allow oral rehearsal before writing for students who need speaking-to-writing support.

## Extension Options
- Ask students to add one sentence about a favorite garment, material, or design area.
- Invite students to compare their Week 1 profile with a peer's profile during the teacher session.

## Notes for Future App Implementation
This week would map well to a simple onboarding flow with reading, listening playback, short-answer writing, and a teacher-reviewed speaking note. The writing activity should preserve sentence frames, bilingual instructions, and visible success criteria.
