# Week 7: Fabric and Texture Basics

## Overview
Week 7 introduces the English students need to describe what a fabric is like. After Week 6, students can already describe color, shape, line, and silhouette. This week shifts from visual look description to textile observation: what the fabric is, how it feels, how it looks, how it moves, and what it is good for.

The main written artifact stays A2-first. Students do not write a paragraph and do not work toward a base word count. Instead, they complete a scaffolded Fabric Swatch Description Card with 6 required sentence-frame lines and 1 optional challenge sentence. This keeps the task concrete, reviewable, and aligned with early Module 1 writing guardrails.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize and name common fabric and material words in English.
- Identify basic texture and surface qualities such as `soft`, `rough`, `smooth`, `shiny`, and `matte`.
- Identify simple weight, movement, structure, and visibility words such as `lightweight`, `heavy`, `stretchy`, `stiff`, `flowy`, `structured`, `transparent`, and `opaque`.
- Read short fabric-swatch descriptions and connect them to the correct keyed images.
- Listen to short spoken fabric descriptions and identify the correct swatch or key detail.
- Use simple frames such as `It is made of ...`, `It feels ...`, `It looks ...`, `It is good for ...`, and `It can be used for ...`.
- Complete a scaffolded fabric-description card with 6 required lines and 1 optional challenge line.
- Describe one fabric orally in 4 to 6 short sentences during the teacher session.

## Professional Fashion-Design Context
Week 7 connects English to introductory sewing, early textiles observation, garment planning, and design reasoning. Fashion Design students need this language when they discuss swatches, choose materials for a garment, compare comfort and movement, and explain why one fabric suits a dress, jacket, shirt, or detail better than another.

The week also prepares students for later work on textiles, sustainability, garment function, styling, portfolio captions, and collection concepts. The focus should stay concrete and observable: what the fabric is made of, how it feels, how it looks, and what it can be used for.

## Language Objectives
- Name common material words such as `fabric`, `material`, `fabric swatch`, `cotton`, `denim`, `linen`, `silk`, `wool`, `polyester`, `lace`, and `leather`.
- Recognize support words such as `textile`, `sample`, `knit fabric`, `woven fabric`, `sheer`, and `breathable`.
- Use feel and surface words such as `soft`, `rough`, `smooth`, `shiny`, `matte`, `textured`, and `plain`.
- Use behavior and visibility words such as `lightweight`, `heavy`, `stretchy`, `stiff`, `flowy`, `structured`, `transparent`, and `opaque`.
- Use description frames such as `This is denim`, `It feels soft`, `It looks shiny`, `It is made of cotton`, and `It is good for summer dresses`.
- Use the optional preference frame `I like this fabric because ...` when ready.

## Materials Needed
- One Week 7 opener board with 3 to 4 garments or swatches that clearly suggest different materials.
- One common-materials swatch board with visible keys.
- One texture-and-qualities board with visible keys.
- One three-swatch reading gallery labeled `image A`, `image B`, and `image C`.
- One reusable four-swatch gallery labeled `image A`, `image B`, `image C`, and `image D`.
- One short listening audio clip or teacher read-aloud fallback for the listening task.
- Visible sentence frames for the Week 7 language focus.
- Student writing space for the Fabric Swatch Description Card.
- Optional classroom support: real swatches or fabric samples for the teacher session.

## Suggested Sequence
1. `m01w07-a01` Welcome Back: students bridge from Week 6 visual language into Week 7 material language with a short opener.
2. `m01w07-a02` Fabric Vocabulary: students complete the main common-materials matching set, then use a short Portuguese bridge or support item.
3. `m01w07-a03` Texture and Material Qualities: students complete the main qualities matching set and use one support item after the core matching.
4. `m01w07-a04` Read: students match three short swatch descriptions to `image A`, `image B`, or `image C` and answer detail questions.
5. `m01w07-a05` Listen: students hear four short fabric descriptions and choose the matching image or key detail.
6. `m01w07-a06` Phrase Practice: students complete fill-blank frames using `made of`, `feels`, `looks`, and `good for`.
7. `m01w07-a07` Fabric Swatch Description Card: students choose one swatch and complete a teacher-reviewed structured writing card.
8. `m01w07-a08` Review and Improve: students correct one common Week 7 sentence problem.
9. `m01w07-a09` Teacher Session: students practice pronunciation and describe one fabric in short supported sentences.

## Differentiation Options
- Keep the swatch boards and galleries visible during vocabulary, reading, listening, writing, and speaking.
- Reduce the number of active production words for learners who need more support and keep `textile`, `breathable`, and `flexible` as recognition words only.
- Let students choose from two easier swatches before moving to a more complex swatch for the writing task.
- Provide a reduced word bank for the writing card if students need more support with visibility or movement words.
- Let students point to the swatch while speaking in the teacher session.

## Extension Options
- Ask stronger learners to add the optional challenge sentence with a clearer design reason.
- Invite stronger learners to add one short comparison such as `This fabric is lighter than denim.` during speaking only if the teacher wants light extension.
- In the teacher session, ask one follow-up question about weather, comfort, or garment type.

## Notes for Future App Implementation
Week 7 should stay strongly image-first and tightly aligned across tasks in a future app. The app-facing flow should preserve:

- one opener board for fast bridge and preview work
- one main common-materials matching set as the primary saved vocabulary output
- one main texture-and-qualities matching set as the primary saved visual-vocabulary output
- one reading gallery with explicit visible keys `image A`, `image B`, and `image C`
- one listening task whose audio script matches the four-swatch gallery and answer key exactly
- one fill-blank phrase-practice task where the primary saved output stays phrase completion
- one teacher-reviewed structured writing card with separate sentence-frame fields instead of a paragraph box
- one teacher-observed speaking checkpoint that shows the gallery and frames but does not record audio

The Week 7 four-swatch gallery should stay reusable for listening, writing, and speaking, and should use the same fabric set and visible image keys across those tasks.
