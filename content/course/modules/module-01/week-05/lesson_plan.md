# Week 5: Body, Figure, and Observation Drawing

## Overview
Week 5 moves from garment-only language into fashion-figure observation language. Students already know garment names and garment parts from Weeks 3 and 4. Now they begin to describe the drawn figure that wears the garment. The week stays visual, concrete, and A2-first. Students review one familiar figure image, learn body-reference and figure-sketch vocabulary, label a front-view figure, listen to pose descriptions, read short sketch captions, practice reliable phrase frames, build a scaffolded Fashion Figure Description Card, review one correction target, and describe one sketch in a teacher-supported speaking session.

The main written artifact is not a paragraph. Students complete a short structured Fashion Figure Description Card with 5 to 6 required sentence-frame lines and 1 optional challenge line. This keeps the task professional and useful without asking A2 learners for long free writing.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize and name basic body-reference points in English.
- Match body-reference and figure-sketch visuals to English vocabulary with Portuguese support when needed.
- Label one fashion figure with clear point-key support.
- Understand short spoken descriptions of pose, view, and garment details.
- Read short sketch captions and match them to visible figures.
- Use sentence frames such as `The figure is ...`, `The figure is wearing ...`, `This is a front view`, `The silhouette is ...`, and `The hem is below the knee`.
- Complete a short Fashion Figure Description Card using 5 to 6 required lines and 1 optional challenge line.
- Revise one sentence focused on missing `is`, `is wearing`, adjective order, or location language.
- Describe one fashion figure or sketch in 4 to 6 short spoken sentences during the teacher session.

## Professional Fashion-Design Context
Week 5 connects English to observation drawing and fashion figure work. Fashion Design students often need to talk about the figure, the pose, the silhouette, and the visible garment on the figure before they move into fuller critique or portfolio writing. This language supports croquis reading, sketch description, early silhouette discussion, and future work in drawing, styling, and design presentation.

The body-reference language in this week should stay respectful and design-focused. Students are not evaluating a real person’s body. They are naming points on a drawn figure and describing pose, proportion, garment fit, silhouette, and visible design features.

## Language Objectives
- Name body-reference points such as `head`, `neck`, `shoulder`, `arm`, `waist`, `hip`, `leg`, `knee`, and `foot`.
- Use figure and sketch vocabulary such as `figure`, `sketch`, `croquis`, `outline`, `pose`, `proportion`, and `silhouette`.
- Use view and direction language such as `front view`, `side view`, `back view`, `standing`, `walking`, and `sitting`.
- Use guided description frames such as `The figure is standing`, `The figure is wearing a dress`, `The silhouette is long and simple`, and `The outline is clear`.
- Use location phrases such as `above the knee` and `below the knee` with obvious visual support.
- Improve adjective order in simple design phrases such as `simple outline`, `long silhouette`, and `relaxed pose`.

## Materials Needed
- One fashion figure opener board for review and preview.
- One body-reference-points diagram with clear labeled points.
- One figure-sketch vocabulary board or croquis concept board.
- One single front-view figure image with explicit point keys for labeling.
- One reusable four-figure gallery for listening, reading, writing, and speaking.
- One short audio clip or teacher read-aloud fallback for the listening task.
- Visible sentence frames for the Week 5 language focus.
- Student writing space for the Fashion Figure Description Card.

## Suggested Sequence
1. `m01w05-a01` Welcome Back: students review garments and notice the move from garment language to figure language.
2. `m01w05-a02` Body and Figure Vocabulary: students complete the two main image-match sets, then use a short Portuguese bridge and one fill-blank item as support.
3. `m01w05-a03` Label the Fashion Figure: students match body-reference words to explicit point keys on one front-view figure.
4. `m01w05-a04` Listen: students hear four short pose-and-view descriptions and choose the correct image or detail answer.
5. `m01w05-a05` Read: students read four sketch captions and match them to the four-figure gallery.
6. `m01w05-a06` Phrase Practice: students complete controlled frames with pose, view, garment, silhouette, outline, and hem-location language.
7. `m01w05-a07` Fashion Figure Description Card: students choose one figure and complete a structured writing card with required lines.
8. `m01w05-a08` Review and Improve: students correct one common Week 5 sentence problem.
9. `m01w05-a09` Teacher Session: students practice pronunciation, name body-reference points, and describe one sketch with support.

## Differentiation Options
- Keep the body-reference diagram and four-figure gallery visible during vocabulary, listening, reading, and writing.
- Reduce the required labeling task to 6 points before adding the optional extra points.
- Provide a reduced word bank for the Fashion Figure Description Card if students need stronger support.
- Let students choose between a simpler front-view figure and a more complex walking figure based on confidence.
- During speaking, allow learners to point to the image while they speak.

## Extension Options
- Ask stronger learners to use the optional challenge line with `outline`, `body line`, or one extra reason sentence.
- Invite stronger learners to compare two figures with one short contrast sentence.
- In the teacher session, ask one follow-up question about silhouette, pose, or garment fit.

## Notes for Future App Implementation
Week 5 should remain image-first and highly aligned across tasks in a future app. The app-facing flow should preserve:

- one opener board for quick review and preview
- two main image-word matching sets as the primary saved vocabulary output
- one figure-labeling task with explicit point keys such as `A-H`
- one listening task whose audio script matches the image gallery and answer key exactly
- one reading task that reuses the same four-figure gallery with explicit image keys such as `image A`, `image B`, `image C`, and `image D`
- one phrase-practice task centered on fill-blank sentence frames as the main saved output
- one teacher-reviewed structured writing card with separate sentence-frame fields instead of a paragraph box
- one teacher-observed speaking checkpoint that displays prompts and tracks completion without recording audio

The Week 5 four-figure gallery should stay reusable in later weeks about sketch description, color, line, silhouette, and design feedback.
