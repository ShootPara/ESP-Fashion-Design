# Week 13: Giving Simple Design Feedback

## Overview
Week 13 helps students move from describing and explaining a design to responding to someone else's work. Students learn how to say what works well, explain why, give one polite suggestion, and state one revision step in simple English.

The main written artifact stays A2-first. Students do not write a paragraph and do not work toward a word-count target. Instead, they complete a scaffolded Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan Card with 6 required sentence-frame lines and 1 optional challenge line. Line 6 uses one stable pattern: `This will make the design more ___.` The optional challenge line is not required for completion and should be saved separately only if the student chooses to complete it. This keeps the writing concrete, teacher-reviewable, and aligned with the Module 1 guardrails while still preparing students for Week 14.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize and use core feedback words such as `feedback`, `comment`, `suggestion`, `revision`, and `strength`.
- Match reusable English feedback phrases to Portuguese support and to their feedback function.
- Distinguish between a positive comment, a reason, a suggestion, and a revision plan.
- Read four short feedback comments and match them to the correct image key.
- Listen to four numbered spoken feedback comments and identify the correct image, strength, or suggestion.
- Complete sentence frames with `because`, `could`, and revision verbs.
- Complete a scaffolded Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan Card with 6 required lines and 1 optional challenge line.
- Give one short positive comment and one short suggestion aloud during the teacher session.

## Professional Fashion-Design Context
Week 13 connects English to beginner studio critique, sketch review, caption revision, and peer response. Students are not doing advanced design criticism. They are learning how to respond to a look, sketch, or mini board in a respectful, useful, and beginner-friendly way.

This week also prepares students for the Week 14 mini design board. The feedback structure matters professionally: identify one strength, explain why it works, suggest one improvement, and say one next revision step.

## Language Objectives
- Use positive feedback frames such as `I like the ...`, `The strongest part is ...`, and `This works well`.
- Use reason language with `because`, `This creates ...`, and `This makes ...`.
- Use polite suggestion frames such as `You could ...`, `Maybe you could ...`, and `Try using ...`.
- Use revision language such as `I will revise ...`, `I will add ...`, `I will change ...`, `clarify`, and `simplify`.
- Use design focus words such as `color palette`, `silhouette`, `detail`, `fabric`, `contrast`, `balanced`, and `clear`.

## Materials Needed
- One Week 13 opener design board with one clear adult-oriented fashion look or sketch.
- One reusable Week 13 gallery labeled only `image A`, `image B`, `image C`, and `image D`.
- One Week 13 listening clip aligned exactly to the reusable gallery.
- Visible sentence frames for positive comment, reason, suggestion, and revision-plan language.
- Student writing space for the Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan Card.
- Optional classroom support: printed gallery cards, projected gallery view, or teacher-selected critique examples.

## Suggested Sequence
1. `m01w13-a01` Welcome Back: students look at one feedback-ready image and answer five short visible-clue questions about what works well.
2. `m01w13-a02` Feedback Phrase Bank: students complete the main phrase matching set and short phrase-function support matches.
3. `m01w13-a03` Positive, Reason, Suggestion, or Revision Plan?: students classify eight short feedback sentence cards.
4. `m01w13-a04` Read: students use the `image A` to `image D` gallery, read four short feedback comments, and answer image-match and detail questions.
5. `m01w13-a05` Listen: students use the same gallery, listen to one numbered audio clip, and answer image and suggestion questions that match the script exactly.
6. `m01w13-a06` Build Better Feedback: students complete six feedback frames with `because`, `could`, and revision language.
7. `m01w13-a07` Design Feedback Note + Revision Plan Card: students choose one gallery image and complete a teacher-reviewed structured writing card with 6 required lines plus 1 optional challenge line saved separately only if completed.
8. `m01w13-a08` Review and Improve: students correct two short Week 13 feedback sentences and save the improved versions.
9. `m01w13-a09` Teacher Session: students practice pronunciation and give one short mini critique using the selected image and saved card from `m01w13-a07` as read-only support.

## Differentiation Options
- Keep Portuguese instructions visible while students produce English answers.
- Keep the reusable image A to image D gallery visible during reading, listening, writing, and speaking tasks.
- Limit the first pass to four core feedback frames: `I like ...`, `because`, `You could ...`, and `I will revise ...`.
- Let students choose the simplest gallery image for the writing card if they need a clearer critique target.
- During the speaking task, allow students to read from the saved feedback card before speaking more freely.

## Extension Options
- Ask stronger learners to use the optional challenge line with `Maybe you could ... because ...`.
- Ask stronger learners to add one extra sentence using `This creates ...` or `This makes the look ...`.
- Reuse the Week 13 gallery as a feedback warm-up before students start Week 14 mini design board revision.

## Notes for Future App Implementation
Week 13 should preserve one stable interaction flow from recognition to production:

- one opener image board for a fast visible-clue critique review
- one main phrase-matching task where matching remains the primary saved output
- one single-choice classification task with student-facing category labels only
- one reading task using a fixed visible-key strategy `image A` to `image D`
- one listening task whose numbered audio script matches the same `image A` to `image D` gallery and answer key exactly
- one fill-blank language task where the primary saved output stays sentence completion
- one teacher-reviewed structured writing card with separate required line fields plus one optional line saved separately instead of a paragraph box
- one sentence-correction task with explicit accepted answers
- one teacher-observed speaking checkpoint that shows the previously selected image and saved writing card from `m01w13-a07` as read-only support and does not record audio

The reusable gallery should stay consistent across reading, listening, writing, and speaking so students do not need a new image set when they move into the main written and spoken artifacts.
