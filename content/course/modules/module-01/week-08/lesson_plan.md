# Week 8: Sewing Actions

## Overview
Week 8 turns fabric and tool vocabulary into process language. After Week 7, students can already talk about fabrics, texture, and material use. This week moves into simple sewing workflow: what action happens first, what tool is used, where the student sews, and how the process finishes.

The main written artifact stays A2-first. Students do not write a paragraph and do not work toward a base word count. Instead, they complete a scaffolded Simple Sewing Process Card with 6 required sentence-frame lines and 1 optional challenge sentence. This keeps the task concrete, reviewable, and aligned with early Module 1 writing guardrails.

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize and name common sewing-action words in English.
- Connect familiar studio tools to simple sewing actions.
- Read a short step-by-step hem process and answer clear sequence and detail questions.
- Listen to a short sewing sequence and identify the correct next step, tool, and final action.
- Use imperative process language such as `Cut the fabric`, `Fold the edge`, and `Check the stitches`.
- Use sequence words such as `first`, `then`, `next`, `after that`, and `finally`.
- Complete a scaffolded process card with 6 required lines and 1 optional challenge line.
- Explain one simple sewing process orally in 5 to 6 short supported steps during the teacher session.

## Professional Fashion-Design Context
Week 8 connects English to introductory sewing workflow, early garment construction, and beginner studio instructions. Fashion Design students need this language when they follow a demonstration, prepare fabric, describe a sewing sample, or explain how they completed a simple classroom task such as marking, cutting, folding, pinning, sewing, and pressing.

The week also prepares students for later work on patternmaking, prototyping, and more detailed process writing. The focus should stay concrete and observable: what the student does, what tool they use, what part of the fabric they work on, and how they check the result.

## Language Objectives
- Name core sewing verbs such as `cut`, `fold`, `pin`, `sew`, `press`, `measure`, `mark`, and `check`.
- Recognize support verbs such as `trace`, `align`, `hold`, `place`, and `remove`.
- Name sewing objects such as `seam`, `stitch`, `hem`, `edge`, `thread`, `needle`, `line`, `scissors`, `pins`, `sewing machine`, `iron`, and `measuring tape`.
- Use sequence words such as `first`, `then`, `next`, `after that`, and `finally`.
- Use location and direction phrases such as `along the line`, `on the edge`, `at the hem`, `on the right side`, and `on the wrong side`.
- Use process-quality support words such as `straight`, `even`, `careful`, and `correct`.

## Materials Needed
- One Week 8 sewing workspace opener board with visible keys.
- One sewing-actions vocabulary board labeled with visible keys `A` to `J`.
- One tools-and-actions board for controlled tool phrase practice.
- One simple hem reading sequence board labeled with visible keys `A` to `F`.
- One reusable sewing-process gallery labeled `image A`, `image B`, `image C`, `image D`, and `image E`.
- One short listening audio clip or teacher read-aloud fallback for the listening task.
- Visible sentence frames for Week 8 process language.
- Student writing space for the Simple Sewing Process Card.
- Optional classroom support: scissors, measuring tape, pins, a sample seam or hem, and fabric pieces for live demonstration.

## Suggested Sequence
1. `m01w08-a01` Welcome Back: students bridge from Week 7 fabric and tool language into Week 8 sewing actions with one compact opener board.
2. `m01w08-a02` Sewing Action Vocabulary: students complete the main sewing-actions matching set, then use a short Portuguese bridge and one support sentence after the core matching.
3. `m01w08-a03` Tools + Actions: students complete controlled `Use ___ to ___` fill-blank phrases linked to the tools board.
4. `m01w08-a04` Read: students read a short hem process, use the keyed step board, and answer first-step, next-step, tool, and detail questions.
5. `m01w08-a05` Listen: students hear one short sewing sequence and answer explicit first, next, final, tool, and detail questions using the shared `image A` to `image E` process gallery.
6. `m01w08-a06` Phrase Practice: students complete fill-blank frames using sequence words, imperative action phrases, and `check ___`.
7. `m01w08-a07` Simple Sewing Process Card: students choose one simple process path from the shared gallery and complete a teacher-reviewed structured writing card.
8. `m01w08-a08` Review and Improve: students correct two short Week 8 process sentences and save the improved versions.
9. `m01w08-a09` Teacher Session: students practice pronunciation and explain one simple sewing process in short supported steps.

## Differentiation Options
- Keep the process boards and galleries visible during reading, listening, writing, and speaking.
- Reduce the active production verbs to `cut`, `fold`, `pin`, `sew`, `press`, and `check` for learners who need more support.
- Let students choose the simpler hem process path rather than the stitch-sample path for the writing card.
- Provide a reduced word bank for the writing card if students need more support with tool phrases or sequence words.
- Let students point to the gallery while speaking in the teacher session.

## Extension Options
- Ask stronger learners to add the optional challenge sentence with a clear process reason.
- Invite stronger learners to add one extra quality sentence in speaking such as `The line should be straight and even.`
- In the teacher session, ask one short follow-up question about tool choice, safety, or why the final check matters.

## Notes for Future App Implementation
Week 8 should stay tightly aligned across image keys, process steps, and listening answers in a future app. The app-facing flow should preserve:

- one opener board for fast bridge and preview work
- one main sewing-actions matching set as the primary saved vocabulary output
- one controlled `Use ___ to ___` fill-blank task where the primary saved output stays phrase completion
- one reading task with a fixed visible-key strategy `A` to `F`
- one listening task whose audio script matches the shared process gallery and answer key exactly
- one fill-blank phrase-practice task where the primary saved output stays sentence completion
- one teacher-reviewed structured writing card with separate sentence-frame fields instead of a paragraph box
- one teacher-observed speaking checkpoint that shows the process gallery and frames but does not record audio

The Week 8 reusable process gallery should stay consistent across listening, writing, and speaking, and should use the same process path and visible image keys across those tasks.
